How These Models Work — Methodology & Derivation

All three intelligence models below are built in-house by Aspire Morning Market Desk from end-of-day market data. They are fully rule-based and transparent — no black box. Each translates raw numbers into a plain-language read of market condition.

★ Aspire Market Health Index (AMHI)

A single 0–100 score blending six weighted technical pillars into one read of overall market health. Computed for every session, so it can be tracked over time and ranked against its own recent history.

  • Trend · 25% — Nifty position vs its 20 / 50 / 200-day moving averages
  • Breadth · 20% — share of advancing vs declining stocks
  • Participation · 15% — % of stocks above their 40-week EMA (large / mid / small)
  • Volatility · 15% — India VIX, inverted (lower VIX = healthier)
  • Flows · 15% — net FII + DII cash activity
  • Momentum · 10% — Nifty 20-session rate of change

Bands: 70+ Strong · 55–70 Constructive · 45–55 Neutral · 30–45 Weak · <30 Fragile. The percentile line compares today's reading to its own last 90 sessions.

◆ Market Regime

A rule-based classification of each session into one of five states, derived from trend structure, breadth and volatility. The timeline strip shows how the regime has shifted across recent sessions.

  • Risk-On — strong uptrend, broad participation, low volatility
  • Constructive — uptrend intact with healthy participation
  • Range — mixed signals, no clear directional edge
  • Distribution — index holding but internals weakening (a caution state)
  • Risk-Off — downtrend, weak breadth or elevated volatility

Because the rules are fixed and visible, every label can be explained — the regime never relies on opaque modelling.

△ Divergence Radar

Scans the last 10 sessions to detect where price and market internals disagree — often the earliest sign that a move is losing or gaining underlying support.

  • Negative Breadth Divergence — price near a 10-day high while breadth weakens
  • Participation Divergence — index near highs but fewer stocks above their 40-week EMA
  • Positive Breadth Divergence — price near a 10-day low while breadth improves
  • Flows Divided — FII and DII positioned on opposite sides

When internals confirm price, the radar says so explicitly — absence of a warning is itself a signal.
